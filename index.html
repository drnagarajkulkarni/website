About

Translating discoveries into therapies for patients.

I am a scientist, drug discovery leader and science communicator, and I have spent close to two decades searching for new medicines. My career has spanned academia, global pharmaceutical research and AI-driven drug discovery, with one purpose: translating scientific discoveries into therapies that improve patients’ lives.

At Sravathi AI Technology I lead multidisciplinary teams working at the intersection of biology, chemistry, computational science and artificial intelligence. My work has contributed to drug discovery programs in oncology, autoimmune diseases, metabolic disorders and other areas of significant unmet medical need.

Beyond the laboratory, I run Pharmacology Insiders, a community that bridges academia and industry through education, mentorship and scientific dialogue, and I write about the long road from molecule to medicine, including in my debut novel.
