Using AI to Accelerate Drug Discovery

Invited talk at the Industry-Academia Conclave (IAC) 8.0, representing Sravathi AI Technology

Afterwards I spent time with PhD scholars, answering their questions on:

  • Academia and industry
  • Career transitions
