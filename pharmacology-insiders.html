Our mission

To connect, educate, and empower pharmacology professionals by fostering collaboration between academia and industry, accelerating the translation of research into real-world therapeutic solutions.

Connect

Meet pharmacologists, drug discovery scientists and clinicians across academia and industry, and build the relationships that move careers and projects forward.

Educate

Plain-language explainers, practical career guidance and honest discussion of where drug discovery is heading, including the role of AI.

Empower

Mentorship and shared experience for researchers moving from academia to industry, and for professionals growing within it.

Who it is for

  • Students and early-career researchers planning their path into industry
  • Pharmacologists and drug discovery scientists who want to compare notes with peers
  • Clinicians interested in how medicines are discovered and developed
  • Anyone working where biology, chemistry and AI meet

Founded by Dr. Nagaraj Kulkarni, a pharmacologist with close to two decades in drug discovery across academia, pharma, CRO and AI-driven research.
